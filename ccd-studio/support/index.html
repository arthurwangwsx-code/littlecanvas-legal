Support & Help

If the camera or gallery is unavailable

Open iOS Settings > CCDStudio and check Camera, Microphone and Photos permissions. Full-library browsing is optional; you can use the app's recent captures without granting full Photos access.

If a photo or video is missing

Check the capture's save status, Apple Photos, then Files > On My iPhone/iPad > CCDStudio > RecoveredPhotos or RecoveredVideos. The in-app gallery shows cached previews, which may not be originals. Do not uninstall before securing a recovery copy.

Getting further support

This help works offline. For technical support, bug reports or privacy requests, email arthur.wang.wsx@gmail.com. Do not send sensitive photos or recordings unless specifically requested through a secure channel.
