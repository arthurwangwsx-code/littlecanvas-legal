Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

On-device processing

CCDStudio processes the photos and videos you capture or choose on your device. This version has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or app-operated media upload service. The app does not send your media to a developer server.

Permissions and photo access

Camera permission is requested for shooting. Microphone permission is requested when recording with sound. Add-only Photos access saves captures. Browsing all system photos is optional and requests read access only after you choose it; limited access stays limited. The system picker opens only items you select.

Where files are stored

Originals are saved in Apple Photos when permitted. If a save cannot complete, recoverable photos/videos may remain under Files > On My iPhone or iPad > CCDStudio > RecoveredPhotos / RecoveredVideos. Recent previews, thumbnails and gallery metadata stay in the app container. Preview files are not a backup of originals.

Retention and deletion

Clear Preview Cache in the gallery removes app previews, not originals in Photos or Files; some gallery metadata may remain. Delete originals from Photos or Files separately. Deleting the app removes its app-local data, not copies already saved in system Photos. Revoke permissions in iOS Settings.

Apple services and sharing

CCDStudio does not operate a photo cloud service. Apple Photos, iCloud Photos, device backups, and exports or shares you initiate can handle your media according to your Apple settings and relevant service policies.

Updates and questions

This notice describes the current app version. We will update it if features or data handling change. Contact us at arthur.wang.wsx@gmail.com for privacy questions or support.
