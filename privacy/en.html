Privacy

LittleCanvas Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

1. What the app does

LittleCanvas is a local-first drawing and sketching app. It includes canvases, drawing tools, a simplified child mode and optional document scanning, photo selection and file export.

2. Artwork stays on your device

Drawings, strokes, layers, titles and selected media are stored locally in the app's storage. LittleCanvas does not require an account or operate a server for hosting your artwork. There are no developer-operated advertising or analytics services in the current app.

If you intentionally export or share a file, its recipient (such as Files, a cloud provider or another app) may process that copy under its own privacy terms. Backups provided by Apple or by a service you choose are separate from uploads made by LittleCanvas.

3. Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only if you choose to scan paper. The app uses Apple's system photo picker only when you select a photo to insert or reference. Selected content is processed for on-device artwork; LittleCanvas does not upload it to a developer service.

4. Parent verification

Some child-mode operations can require on-device verification through Apple's Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. Authentication is handled by iPadOS/iOS. LittleCanvas does not receive or save biometric templates or your passcode.

5. Tracking and data collection

The current app does not track users or collect personal data for the developer. It does not include a developer-operated analytics platform. Apple independently handles any App Store data, device backups or system diagnostics under its own policies.

6. Family and children

Child mode provides simplified drawing controls. Children's artwork is stored on the device by default. Caregivers should supervise file sharing and access to external services. Please do not publicly post children's private artwork or photos in support requests.

7. Deletion, retention and security

You can manage, move to Trash and delete artwork using the app's controls. Some artwork versions or Trash items may remain until explicitly removed. Removing the app also removes its local app data, subject to device backup and platform behavior. Exported copies must be deleted from the services where you placed them.

We use Apple platform sandboxing and permission controls to limit access to your local files. This policy will be updated if the app introduces developer-operated accounts, sync, analytics or other new processing.

8. Contact

To ask a privacy or support question, visit the LittleCanvas Support page. You can open an issue using the official public support repository. Do not include personal data or children's images in public issues.
